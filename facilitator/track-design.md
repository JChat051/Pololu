# Track Design

## Materials

- Matt **white** surface: large sheets of white card/paper, a white-painted board, or a white vinyl/roller banner printed blank. **Matt** matters; glossy surfaces reflect glare and confuse the sensors.
- **Black electrical tape**, about 19 mm wide (standard PVC tape). Matt black is best.
- Masking tape to fix the card to the floor, so it does not slide when robots turn.
- Marker pen and a long piece of string for drawing smooth curves.

## Guidelines

- Keep **lines about 19-25 mm wide** and avoid shiny tape.
- Use **smooth curves**. Aim for a radius of at least 15 cm for Track A. Sharp corners are hard for this example.
- Keep the track **away from direct sunlight and spotlights** as strong infrared light can disturb readings.
- Leave **at least 20 cm** of white border around the line.
- Lay tape in one continuous strip where possible; overlap ends neatly.
- Make at least **one track per 3-4 teams** so nobody waits too long.

## Track A: The oval (warm-up, baseline)

```
        ______________________
      /                        \
     |                          |
     |                          |
      \________________________/
```

About 1.2 m x 0.8 m, gentle curves, one lap is roughly 3 metres. Used for Challenges 0-3.

## Track B: The S-bend (stretch and race)

```
      ____________
     /            \____________
    |                          \
    |        ______             |
    |       /      \____        |
     \_____/            \______/
```

Includes an S-bend and one tighter curve (radius about 10-12 cm). Used for Challenge 4 and the showcase race. Keep corners smooth: sharp 90-degree corners and crossings are out of scope for this activity.

## Marking a start line

Add a short cross-line of masking tape (not black) beside the track as the start/finish for timing. Use a phone stopwatch or a facilitator with a clipboard.

## Dry-run notes

Record on the plan sheet the **safe**, **fast** and **break-point** speeds for your robot edition on each track, so you can guide teams who get stuck.
